About QuickNotes

"How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open: Swipe up from the bottom-right corner of your iPad screen, or press Globe + Q on a Mac.
  2. Write: Type your thoughts, sketch, or right-click text in Safari to select "Add to Quick Note."
  3. Save: Tap Done to close the window and automatically save it to your Quick Notes folder.

Your notes

You have 2 notes.

  • Instant Activation: Open notes instantly with a corner swipe or a quick keyboard shortcut from any app.
  • Contextual Linking: Remembers what app you were using and lets you link a specific webpage or map location directly into the note.
  • Smart Text Clipping: Highlight text in Safari, right-click, and choose "Add to Quick Note" to auto-clip quotes and sources.
